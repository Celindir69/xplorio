# Xplorio: Rediscover Your Music

*The music collection you already own is bigger than the one you listen to.*

At some point it's there: a collection of thousands of albums, lovingly gathered over the years. And yet you keep
coming back to the same twenty records. Not because the rest is worse, but because long lists from A to Z don't make
you curious.

**Xplorio** is a new interface for your Volumio player, made for exactly these collections. Instead of making you
scroll through endless lists, it takes you on a journey of discovery through your own music.

## You know what you want to hear?

Just type it. Search finds artists, albums, tracks and genres in your collection and, if you like, on TIDAL, Qobuz or
HIGHRESAUDIO too (valid subscription needed). One tap, and it plays.

## Can't decide?

As soon as you open it, Xplorio greets you with the **album of the day**, what you played recently and what is new in
the collection. Or leave the search field empty and let yourself be inspired:

- **Birthdays** celebrate albums released today, this week or this month, with round anniversaries in gold.
- **Random Discoveries** brings up albums that haven't played in a long time.
- **Played a Year Ago** reminds you of what kept you company last autumn.
- **Once Played a Lot** brings back old favourites you had almost forgotten.
- **Never Heard** shows what has been sitting unplayed on the shelf for years.
- **Hidden Gems** finds music you barely know that matches exactly what you love. And Xplorio tells you why:
  "Like Lumen Drift", "Melancholic · Jazz" or "♥ Unheard" from a favourite artist.

Or tap a mood, an energy level, a style or a decade: *Dreamy*, *High energy*, *Soulful*, *the 80s*. You get all
matching albums from your collection, and one tap on the dice plays 25 random tracks from them.

## Looking for music for exactly this moment?

The **Mood Mix** puts together a playlist for you: pick one or more moods, how much energy you want, which genres and
how adventurous the mix should be, from your favourites to the hidden gems. Before anything plays, you see every track
with the reason it was picked. Don't like one? Out it goes.

And when the mix is over? With the optional **AutoDJ**, the journey simply continues, with music that fits what was
just playing.

## Like what's playing right now?

Then start right there. Tap the artist and see what of theirs is in your collection. Look at similar artists you also
own. Read who played on the album and what there is to know about it. Every album page offers similar albums from
your collection, even for albums you are only streaming. Follow your own path through the collection to
the next album that grabs you. And if you can't decide: for artists, genres, moods and decades, the dice plays 25
tracks from them.

Meanwhile the **lyrics** scroll along in sync, the colours adapt to the cover, and a badge shows you the quality of
what's playing.

## Your music, your story

Xplorio remembers what you listen to, and can combine it with your Last.fm history. From that come your charts,
statistics by time of day and weekday, and a **year in review**: which artists did you discover this year? Which genre
got you through the winter?

With a heart for artists, stars for albums and a thumb for single tracks, you tell Xplorio what you like. What you
don't want to hear no longer shows up in random mixes.

## Good data, good discoveries

The better your collection is tagged, the more Xplorio has to tell. That's why a **tag editor** is built in: for
single tracks, whole albums or everything by an artist, including cover search. The **library check** finds missing
covers, different spellings of the same artist and albums without a genre, and takes you straight to the fix with one
tap.

And if you want to go deeper, let a more powerful computer listen to your music: the included **audio analysis**
detects energy, tempo and mood straight from the sound and makes the Mood Mix and Discover even more accurate.

## At home everywhere

On your phone in your hand, on the iPad as a stage next to the hi-fi, on the TV as a pure display with cover and
lyrics. Light or dark, in English or German. And the familiar Volumio interface is always just a tap away.
